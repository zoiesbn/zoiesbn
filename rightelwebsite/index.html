Background & Context

From Homepage to SIM Purchase System

Rightel is one of Iran’s three major mobile operators, serving millions of prepaid and postpaid users. Its homepage is the primary entry point to the digital ecosystem and a major gateway to revenue-generating journeys.

As products and services expanded, the website structure became increasingly complex. Users struggled to discover relevant offerings and reach key actions such as purchasing a SIM card.

The redesign simplified navigation, improved information architecture, and created clearer pathways from exploration to purchase completion.

Navigation & Information Architecture

Problem

The existing architecture reflected internal business structures rather than user goals. Products, services, and customer journeys accumulated into excessive choices and increased cognitive load.

Past Information Architecture

Solution

Navigation was reorganized around user needs and high-frequency tasks. Similar services were consolidated, redundant paths removed, and content grouped around user mental models. The homepage became a clear route into the system rather than a collection of internal departments, with product discovery, navigation, and purchase designed as one continuous customer journey.
