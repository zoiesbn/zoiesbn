About Me

I’m Zoha, a product designer with a strong interest in creating thoughtful and user-centered experiences. I enjoy working in collaborative teams where ideas can grow through discussion and feedback.

Outside of design, I’m usually into sports, good food, movies, and books.

I like observing how stories are told—whether in a film, a book, or a product—and bringing that sense of clarity and experience into my design work.
